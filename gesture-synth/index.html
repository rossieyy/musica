How to Play

Gesture Synth is an interactive musical instrument. Use your hands to create chords, melodies, and sounds in real-time!

🎵 Pitch Shift (Left Hand)

  • Top 50% of screen → Raise 1 semitone
  • Middle 30% → Normal pitch
  • Bottom 20% → Lower 1 semitone
